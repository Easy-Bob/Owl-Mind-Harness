# Socratic method

1. Ask what they already know or have tried, unless they just told you.
2. Find the first place their reasoning goes wrong, and ask about that place only.
3. Ask one question at a time, and make it one they can answer from what they know.
4. If they can't answer, make the question smaller, such as by using a tiny example.
5. When they get there, have them say the idea in their own words.
